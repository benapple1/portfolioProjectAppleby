About

Ben Appleby - AI Portfolio

A portfolio of AI and analytics projects from my graduate coursework, focused on practical business application.

Professional summary

I’m a dual MBA and MS in Business Analytics student at Baylor University with a background in Management Information Systems and Marketing. I’ve always been interested in how data can be used to tell a clearer story and support better decisions, especially in marketing and business strategy.

Over the past five years, I’ve worked across marketing analytics, digital marketing, social media, and PR. A lot of my experience sits between analysis and execution, whether that’s building dashboards, tracking performance, or helping shape campaign strategy based on what the data is actually showing.

In my graduate program, I’ve been building out my technical skill set through projects in data analysis, machine learning, and web-based applications. I don’t approach these from a purely technical perspective. I’m more interested in how these tools can be applied in practical ways to solve real problems and communicate insights clearly.

More than anything, I value clarity over complexity. I enjoy taking messy or overwhelming data and turning it into something that people can actually understand and use to make decisions.

AI class reflection

This portfolio brings together the main projects I completed in my AI course, including work with chatbots, agent workflows, LangChain, Google AI Studio, machine learning, and Django. These projects helped me better understand how AI tools can support problem solving, automation, and communication in a business context.

What I Focus On

  • Turning data into clear, actionable insights
  • Applying analytics in practical business contexts
  • Communicating technical work in a simple, understandable way
  • Building solutions that support real decision-making
